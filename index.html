How to play
  • Left thumb anywhere on the left side: move.
  • Shoot: hold to power up, let go to strike. Aim with the stick.
  • Pass: passes toward where you're pointing.
  • Without the ball, Shoot becomes Sprint (and tackles harder) and Pass becomes Switch.
  • 3-minute match. Walls around the pitch, no offside.
